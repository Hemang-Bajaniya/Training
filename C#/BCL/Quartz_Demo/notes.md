-- Quartz.NET is an open‑source job scheduling library for C# that allows developers to schedule and run tasks (jobs) at specific times or intervals, much like cron jobs in Linux. 

Quartz.NET works by combining three main components:

    IJob – Represents the unit of work to be performed.

    ITrigger – Defines the schedule or timing of the job.

    IScheduler – Manages and coordinates job execution.
